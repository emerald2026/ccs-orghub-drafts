About CCS OrgHub

Our Mission & Vision

Guiding the College of Computer Studies towards a connected, efficient, and digitally-driven student community.

Our Mission

To provide a centralized, efficient, and user-friendly digital platform that streamlines student organization workflows, enhances communication, and empowers student leaders in the College of Computer Studies.

We are committed to replacing manual, paper-based processes with modern, accessible technology that serves both students and faculty.

Our Vision

To be the leading student organization portal in the university, fostering a vibrant, engaged, and digitally-driven community that supports the holistic development of every CCS student.

We envision a future where administrative tasks are seamless, allowing students to focus on innovation, leadership, and academic excellence.
